# EMP - Entrepreneurship - NICE Headphones: High-Quality Modular Audio Solution

### Project Description: 
Developed for the *Entrepreneurship* course, this project outlines a comprehensive business plan for **NICE Lda.**, a startup designing modular, high-fidelity headphones at an affordable price (€100). The plan demonstrates market viability through product innovation, strategic positioning, financial modeling, and operational planning.

### Key Features

#### 1. Product Innovation

- **Modular Design:** Interchangeable drivers, ear cushions, and headbands for customization and repair.
- **Audio Tuning:** High-quality sound co-developed with ISEL’s Audio Lab (LAA-ISEL partnership).
- **Eco-Friendly:** Supports circular economy with replaceable components to reduce waste.

#### 2. Market Strategy

- **Target Audience:** Gamers, music lovers, and budget-conscious audiophiles.
- **Price Point:** Positioned at €100 to bridge the gap between entry-level and premium headphones.
- **Distribution Channels:**
  - Online store and retail partnerships (e.g., Worten, Fnac)
  - Collaborations with influencers (musicians, streamers)
  - In-store demo booths in shopping centers

#### 3. Financial Plan

- **Revenue Model:**
  - Unit sales target: 1,560 units/year (Year 1), with 5% annual growth
  - After-sales income from component replacements

- **Cost Structure:**
  - 50% production cost (outsourced assembly)
  - Marketing: €640/month (influencers + promotions)

- **Funding Sources:**
  - €8,000 from founders
  - €14,000 loan (4 years @ 3.5% interest)

#### 4. Operational Workflow

- **Supply Chain:** Components sourced from multiple suppliers; final assembly handled by third-party partners.
- **Sales Pipeline:** Orders processed through a call center and shipped via logistics providers.
